Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tealogy Cafe? +
Tealogy Cafe is a cozy, modern cafe offering a wide variety of teas, coffees, and fresh snacks. We blend tradition with innovation to create a unique experience for every guest.
Do you offer free Wi-Fi? +
Yes! We offer complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi for all our customers. Just ask our staff for the password when you visit.
Can I bring my own cake for a special occasion? +
Absolutely! You are welcome to bring your own cake for birthdays or celebrations. Please inform our staff in advance so we can assist you.
How can I give feedback? +
We love hearing from you! Please use the feedback form at the bottom of this page or speak to our staff. You can also email us at feedback@tealogycafe.com.
